Choose the *Furniture* tool, tap a room, and pick from the library on the right. New pieces appear in the middle of the room.

![Furniture from several packs in a room](new-furniture.jpg)

## The library

- It has sections (lights, living, dining, kitchen, sleeping, bath, work, parking) followed by the **furniture packs**. NextFloor ships 40 basic models and 43 packs with about 800 more.
- The **search** finds German and English names, pack names and several words in any order.
- Furniture is sorted by kind (seating, beds, kitchen …) with a picture and its size. A model that comes in several sizes is one card with size chips.
- With a room selected, what fits it comes first (*Fits …*). *Show all rooms* lifts that.
- Filters: ⚡ can be linked to a device, 💡 glows, 🌳 outdoors. ★ Favourites and *Recently used* sit on top.

## Move, resize, copy

- Drag to move, drag the corners to resize, use the turn handle, or type position, size and rotation.
- **Mirror** flips left and right. **Duplicate** copies. **Fix** locks a piece. **Move to floor** takes it to the same spot on another floor.
- **Resizing** keeps legs, arms, plates and handles at their real size; only the middle grows.
- **Tilt:** *Tilt front/back* and *Tilt sideways* (degrees) lean a ladder, tilt a picture or follow a sloped shelf. Owned 3D models are not tilted.
- **Place spots:** a room's *Place spots* lays out a grid of lamps (columns × rows) that all follow one light, undoable in one step.
- **Wall view (▣):** in a room's wall list, *Wall view* shows a wall from the front with its doors, windows and the furniture against it. Drag, or type the distance and height.

## The furniture studio

*🛠️ Build your own piece* sits at the top of the library.

1. Pick a template: cabinet, shelf, lamp or device.
2. Type the measurements in centimetres and set doors, drawers, boards, handles, base and colours. The front view follows every change.
3. *Put in the plan* stores it in the pack *Own furniture*, so it also shows in the library.

To change it later, select the piece and press *Change in the studio*. Every copy in the plan takes the new size.

> [!TIP]
> Need something the studio cannot make? The browser-based [furniture builder](https://therealmrbk.github.io/NextFloor/builder/) builds a model exactly as NextFloor draws it, and you can share it. See [Furniture packs](packs.md).

## Special pieces

### Lamps

Lamps are furniture with a light behind them; the lights section has twelve kinds. Link them as described in [Connect devices](devices.md).

### Light switches and sockets

In the smart home pack, 1- to 4-gang. Each button has its own entity and glows while what it switches is on.

### Awnings, curtains, blinds, screens and gates

*Moves with* picks the cover entity the piece follows. *Reverse the direction* turns it round for things that move the opposite way in the plan than in reality.

### Sofas

The built-in sofa has a *Shape*: straight, L (mirror it for the other side) or U.

### Picture frames

From the pack *Pictures & frames*: upload a photo in the form, type a picture address (`/local/…`) or pick an image or camera entity. The mat and whether the photo fills the frame are adjustable.

### Show only while …

Any piece can wait for an entity: the Christmas tree only while a helper is on. Use several states separated by commas, or the other way round. The editor always shows the piece.

### Under the roof slope

Ceiling lamps and hanging pieces in an attic room have *📐 Up to the roof slope*.

### Seasonal decoration

The packs *Winter & Christmas* and *Halloween* come with glowing parts that follow a light or switch you link. Combine them with *Show only while …* to show a decoration only in its season.

## Layers and floor meters

- **Layers:** a floor marked *Layer* in its form (the trees, the decoration) gets an eye at the top of the 3D view that shows or hides it.
- **Floor meter:** *Floor meter (power)* in the floor form takes a power sensor for that floor alone. Its value shows on the floor label, and the house card in that floor's view shows it with its share of the house.

## Move the pin

Pins of devices that stand close together can be pushed aside or up in the form; the device itself stays where it is.
