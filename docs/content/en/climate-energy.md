## Heat maps

The map selector in the 3D view colours every room by a value. Besides **Temperature**, **Humidity** and **CO₂** there are:

| Map | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Mould** | the risk at each room's coldest wall, with a comfort score |
| **Values** | the numbers per room, with airing hints |
| **Wi-Fi** | each access point's reach on the floor, its devices, and which network devices are offline |
| **Sun** | the hours of sun each room gets today through its windows |
| **Power** | what each room draws right now |

The temperature scale is a summer scale from May to September and a winter scale otherwise.

> [!NOTE]
> The **sun hours** are worked out from the plan itself: its windows, the compass and the sun's path. Set the [compass](outside.md#compass-and-map) before you trust them.

## In the room panel

- The room's **temperature over the last 24 hours** and when to air it.
- Today's and this month's **kWh and cost** for the room.
- The **sun over the year**, a **shade hint** when the sun heats the room, and a hint when a window stands open while the heating runs.
- **Low batteries** and **lights left on in empty rooms** are collected as hints.
- **Underfloor heating** glows on the floor of rooms where you named its thermostat, valve or switch.

## Energy

The energy features need a **solar field**, a **meter** and an **inverter**. Battery and grid connection are optional. Setup is in [Outside, roof and energy](outside.md#energy).

- **Power flow:** particles run from the solar modules to the inverter, the house, the battery and the grid, along the real [cable routes](outside.md#energy) you drew. A glass card shows the balance and your self-sufficiency.
- **Battery:** a home battery shows its charge on its front.
- **Meters:** gas and water meters add their readings to the balance.
- **Floor meter:** a power sensor per floor shows its value on the floor label and its share of the house.
- **House card:** the energy card over the roof shows the last 24 hours as a curve (solar as an area, consumption as a line) and the last **seven days** as bars. It also stays above an opened floor.
- **Standby eaters:** the plan check lists devices that draw power while idle, with their yearly cost.
