Furniture from the garden and household packs turns everyday things into small live signals.

## Waste bins

Waste bins (garden pack, four colours, 120 and 240 l) take a *Pickup date (sensor)*, for example from Waste Collection Schedule or any sensor with days or a date. The day before and on the day, the bin glows and shows its pin. The *Today* card lists the bins due today or tomorrow.

## Mailbox

Pick the *Post sensor* (for example a contact on the flap). While there is post, the mailbox glows.

## Plants

Set *Soil moisture (sensor)* and *Thirsty below (%)*. Below that value the plant glows.

## Washer, dryer, dishwasher

When the device has a remaining-time sensor in Home Assistant, its pin says "25 min left".

## Pets

Pets (the pets pack: cat, dog, rabbit) walk around their room, round the furniture, with breaks in between. With a **Room sensor** (Bermuda, ESPresense) a pet roams the room it really is in.

## Garden

- **Robot mowers:** a mower or dock from *Garden 2* linked to a `lawn_mower` mows lanes over its lawn while it mows.
- **Sprinklers, pools and ponds:** pools, ponds and beds show water temperature and pump on their pin.
- **Gardens linked to their area:** an outdoor area linked to a Home Assistant area brings its devices into the [Outside panel](outside.md#the-outside-panel).
